Resume Builder

A simple website to build a one-page resume in minutes. No sign-up, no login, just open the site, fill in your details and get a clean resume.

Features
No login required: Start building your resume right away, without creating an account.
4 resume templates: Choose from four different designs and pick the one that suits you.
One-page resume: Your details are arranged neatly on a single page.
Easy form-based input: Fill in your details (personal info, education, experience, skills) and see them in the resume.
Switch templates anytime: Try different templates without re-entering your information.
